Overview

Cockpit is a modular, glanceable dock for visionOS. Pin it in your space and keep essentials within reach, so you can check key info without constant app‑switching.

Getting Started

Place & resize the dock

  • Open Cockpit. A compact dock window appears; place it where you want.
  • Resize from the bottom edge; height stays proportional for clarity.
  • The dock reappears across sessions where you left it.

Add and arrange panels

  • Tap the edit button below the dock.
  • Tap ＋ Add to insert panels you haven’t placed yet.
  • Drag panels to reorder; tap the small × to remove one.
  • Tap the Layout button to switch between a row, two rows, a column, or two columns.

Appearance & theme

  • Tap the palette button in edit mode to choose a theme.
  • 19 themes are available; Midnight, Cockpit, and Minimal are free, the rest are part of Cockpit Pro.
  • The Cockpit instrument theme offers a precise, flight‑deck look.

Useful settings

  • Time format: System / 12‑hour / 24‑hour
  • First day of the week
  • Temperature and wind units
  • Weather refresh interval
  • Panel text size: Standard or Large, for panels with small type
  • Back up or restore your whole layout as a file, from Settings → Backup

Panels

Clock

Large, glanceable time with weekday and date. The Cockpit instrument theme adds an analog dial and a UTC offset readout.

Weather

Current conditions and a compact forecast powered by Apple WeatherKit. Choose °C/°F and wind units; set a refresh interval.

Calendar

Your week, upcoming events, and reminders that are due, at a glance (read‑only, with your permission).

Focus Timer

Quick focus sessions with presets (5/15/30/60 min), a clean progress ring, pause/resume/stop, and a clear completion state.

Now Playing

Apple Music now playing appears only while audio is active on this device—so you always see what’s on.

Apps

One‑tap buttons that open your other apps, each with an SF Symbol icon of your choosing.

Countdown

Days left until a date that matters to you, with a symbol you pick for it.

Daily Goal

One number to reach today—steps, glasses of water, pages—counted with a tap.

Vision Pro Status Cockpit Pro

CPU, memory, storage, battery level/state, thermal state (Normal/Fair/Serious/Critical), Low Power, and uptime—organized as rings and concise rows.

Shortcuts Cockpit Pro

One‑tap buttons that run your Shortcuts by exact name. You can add an SF Symbol icon. Running a Shortcut may briefly open the Shortcuts app; some actions aren’t available on visionOS.

Home Cockpit Pro

Launch HomeKit scenes and toggle accessories directly from the dock. Create/edit scenes in Apple’s Home app. Up to three accessories are listed for quick toggles.

Network Cockpit Pro

Connection type (Wi‑Fi/Ethernet/Cellular/Offline), local IPv4, ping latency (measured to one.one.one.one), and real‑time download/upload throughput sampled every second.

Activity Cockpit Pro

Apple Watch‑style Move, Exercise, and Stand rings plus your daily step count (read‑only via HealthKit). Updates every 5 minutes.

Habits Cockpit Pro

A few daily habits and your current streak, checked off with a tap.

World Clock Cockpit Pro

Local time in the cities that matter to you.

Customization

  • Panels: Add/remove and drag to reorder in edit mode.
  • Layout: Tap the Layout button to choose Row, Two Rows, Column, or Two Columns. The two‑lane grids need a few panels to be worth splitting into.
  • Theme: 19 themes in three families — soft, translucent Glass (Midnight, Graphite, Sunset, Ocean, Holo, Crystal); dense technical Instrument readouts (Cockpit, Apollo, F1, Tron, Outrun, Steampunk, Telemetry, Mecha, Visor); and paper‑bright, editorial Light themes (Minimal, Bauhaus, Zen, Vellum). Midnight, Cockpit, and Minimal are free; the rest are Cockpit Pro.
  • Text size: Settings → Appearance → Panel Text Size (Standard/Large) enlarges the small labels inside panels without changing the large readouts.
  • Resize & scale: Drag the window edge; the dock keeps its aspect ratio so content stays legible.
  • Backup: Settings → Backup saves your whole layout, theme, and panel data to one file, and can restore it later.
  • Persistence: Cockpit remembers your layout and reappears across sessions.

Cockpit Pro

Cockpit is free to use, with a core set of panels and three starting themes. Cockpit Pro unlocks everything else, however you choose to pay for it: Monthly, Yearly (with a free trial for new subscribers), or a one‑time Lifetime purchase.

Free

Clock, Weather, Calendar, Focus Timer, Apps, Countdown, Daily Goal, and Now Playing. Midnight, Cockpit, and Minimal themes.

Cockpit Pro

Vision Pro Status, Shortcuts, Home, Network, Activity, Habits, and World Clock. Every remaining theme.

To upgrade, tap the Cockpit Pro row at the top of Settings. Already purchased on another device with the same Apple Account? Tap Restore Purchases in the same section. A Monthly or Yearly subscription can be managed or cancelled from Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your paired iPhone.

Permissions

Calendar & Reminders

Used to show upcoming events and reminders that are due (read‑only). You can change access anytime: open Cockpit → Settings → System Settings.

Location & WeatherKit

Used to show local weather. You can change access anytime in System Settings.

Health

Read‑only access to Activity rings and steps to populate the Activity panel. No health data leaves your device.

HomeKit

Required to run scenes and toggle accessories you already set up in the Home app.

Apple Music

Used to display what’s currently playing in the Now Playing panel.

You can review every permission Cockpit uses, and jump straight to System Settings to change one, from Cockpit → Settings → Review Permissions.

Troubleshooting

Calendar shows no events or reminders
  • Open Cockpit → Settings → System Settings and allow Calendar access (this also covers reminders that are due).
  • Make sure the calendars you expect are enabled on this device.
Weather is missing or not updating
  • Allow Location access in System Settings.
  • Check your Weather refresh interval in Cockpit Settings.
  • Verify your network connection.
Activity is unavailable or denied
  • Enable Health access in System Settings (read‑only).
  • Ensure your iPhone/Apple Watch is syncing Activity to Health with iCloud.
  • Activity refreshes every ~5 minutes.
Home shows no scenes or accessories
  • Set up Home on your iPhone first, then open Cockpit.
  • Allow Home access in System Settings.
  • Only scenes created in the Home app appear here. Up to three accessories are listed for quick toggles.
Shortcuts don’t run
  • Install Apple’s Shortcuts app on this device.
  • Use the exact Shortcut name when adding a button.
  • Some Shortcut actions aren’t available on visionOS; Shortcuts will show a warning.
Now Playing doesn’t appear
  • The panel appears only while Apple Music is playing on this device.
  • Make sure the Music app is installed and you’re playing locally.
Network shows “—” or no IP
  • If offline, IP and latency may be unavailable.
  • Latency is measured with a lightweight request to one.one.one.one.
  • VPNs or special network setups can affect IP/throughput readings.
Battery shows “—” or values differ from system
  • Sandboxed apps have access only to coarse battery/thermal signals. Cockpit displays what the system exposes to apps.
Panel text is too small to read
  • Open Cockpit → Settings → Appearance and set Panel Text Size to Large.
  • This enlarges the small labels inside every panel; large readouts like the clock, the timer, and the temperature stay the same size on purpose.
  • To make the whole bar bigger instead, drag its corner.
I purchased Cockpit Pro but panels are still locked
  • Open Cockpit → Settings → Cockpit Pro → Restore Purchases.
  • Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • For a Monthly or Yearly plan, confirm the subscription is still active in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your paired iPhone.
The dock is too small/large
  • Resize from the bottom edge of the window. The dock scales proportionally to keep text readable.
